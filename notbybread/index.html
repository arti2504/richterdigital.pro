Not By Bread

Man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of God. Matthew 4:4

A quiet companion for fasting and prayer. When you’re hungry, pray.

  • A prayer for the moment hunger comesTap “I’m struggling” and hear a prayer for this hour of your fast.
  • Fast together with your churchJoin your group with a code, see who is fasting with you and pray for each other’s requests.
  • The Daniel Fast and 21 Days of PrayerA daily Scripture, a short devotional and one question to carry into the evening.
  • A journal of answered prayerWrite down what you ask, and look back at what God did.
Coming to the App Store in December

Did your church give you a code? Open the link they shared, or enter the code in the app under “I have a group code”.

Talk to your doctor before fasting, especially if you take medication or have a health condition. Ending a fast early is never failure.
