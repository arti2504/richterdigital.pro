Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

In short

  • Personal prayers are written by an AI service only after you agree. You can use written prayers instead, which never leave your phone.

2. What stays on your phone

  • Your answers to the health questions before a fast, your weight and your evening check-ins.
  • Your fasts, streak, program progress and settings.
  • Your journal, unless you back it up (see 3f).
  • Your location, if you allow it for a sunrise-to-sunset fast. The app uses it once on the phone to work out today’s sunset and does not send it anywhere.

Morning words, the evening check-in, notes during a fast, the Sunday question and the reminder before a free trial ends are scheduled on your phone. If you delete the app, this data is gone.

3. What leaves your phone, and why

a) Your account

Once you have finished setting up the app, it creates an anonymous account with a random ID at our backend provider, Supabase. If you sign in with Apple, we receive an Apple user ID and, if you choose to share them, your name and email address (or Apple’s private relay address). If you sign in by email, we receive your email address. The account keeps your group, your journal backup and your purchase with you on a new phone.

b) Reminders from your group

We store your time zone and your morning and evening times and, if you allow notifications, your device’s push token, so that notes such as “3 people prayed for you today” arrive at a good local time and never at night.

c) Personal prayers (only with your consent)

Before the first personal prayer the app asks for your permission. If you agree, each time you ask for a prayer the app sends to our server, and from there to Anthropic or OpenAI: your first name, the kind of fast and the hour you are in, the topics you pray about, the time of day, your Bible translation, your church background and the note you typed, if any. Your last five journal entries are included only if you turn that switch on.

Before anything is sent, the app checks your note on the phone for signs of crisis or of a body warning sign. We do not store the text of prayers or notes on our server. We keep a short record that a prayer was made (time, prompt version, provider) to prevent misuse. The AI providers work on our behalf; under their API terms they do not use this data to train their models and may keep it for a limited time to detect abuse. You can turn personal prayers off in Profile at any time.

d) Spoken prayers (paid feature)

To read a prayer aloud, its text, which includes your first name, is sent to OpenAI or ElevenLabs to create the audio. The audio file is kept on our server for up to 24 hours so it can be played again, then deleted.

e) Groups

When you join or create a group we store the first name you give, your membership and role, how many fasts you completed, and whether you are fasting right now and until when. Other members see how many are fasting; your name appears only for a fast where you chose “Let my group see my name”. We also store the prayer requests you post to the group wall, who tapped “praying”, and reports and blocks.

Before a post appears, its text is checked for safety by Anthropic or OpenAI. A post that looks like a possible crisis is not published and is stored without its text; the group leader only learns that someone may need care.

f) Journal backup

If you sign in, your journal entries are backed up so you can restore them on a new phone. The text is stored encrypted. An entry you delete is deleted from the backup too.

g) Purchases

Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details. RevenueCat, Inc. (USA) receives your app user ID and Apple’s purchase receipts to confirm your subscription.

h) Delivering notifications

Group notifications are delivered through the Expo push service (650 Industries, Inc., USA) and Apple’s push service. They receive your push token and the text of the notification.

i) Safety counts

When the crisis screen or the warning-sign card appears, our server counts it without any user ID, device ID or text, so we can see whether our safety checks misfire.

j) Product analytics

We use PostHog (PostHog, Inc., USA) to see which parts of the app are used, with events such as “fast started” or “paywall viewed” and a random device ID. Events never contain your name, notes, prayers, journal, health answers, weight, warning signs, crisis signals or how long you fasted. PostHog is set to discard IP addresses.

4. Sensitive information

Using a Christian prayer and fasting app says something about your faith, and prayer requests can touch on health. We treat all of it as sensitive: we collect only what a feature you choose needs, we never sell it, and we never use it for advertising.

5. Legal bases (GDPR)

Because we are based in Germany, the EU General Data Protection Regulation applies.

  • Providing the app, your account and your subscription: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Personal prayers, the journal switch, groups and notifications: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time in the app; this does not affect what happened before.
  • Safety counts, abuse prevention and product analytics without sensitive data: our legitimate interest in a safe, working app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

6. Where data is processed

Our backend runs on Supabase servers in the United States. The AI, voice, purchase, push and analytics providers named above are based in the United States. Transfers from the EU rely on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or on the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. How long we keep data

  • Account, group data, posts and journal backup: until you delete them or your account.
  • Spoken prayer audio: up to 24 hours.
  • Push token: until you delete your account or turn notifications off.
  • Safety counts: kept, as they contain nothing about you.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Export: Profile → Export my data.
  • Delete: Profile → Delete account removes your account and everything on our server that is linked to you. Records that a prayer was made remain only without any link to you.
  • Personal prayers and journal in prayers: on and off in Profile.
  • Groups: leave at any time; choose per fast whether your name shows.
  • Notifications: in the iPhone Settings.

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, receive a copy of, and object to the processing of your data, and to withdraw consent. Write to us at richterdigitals@gmail.com. You may also complain to a data protection authority; ours is the Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen.

Residents of U.S. states with privacy laws, such as California, have the same rights. We do not sell personal data, do not share it for targeted advertising, and do not use it for profiling with legal or similar effects.

9. Consumer health data (Washington, Nevada, Connecticut)

Consumer health data in this app means: answers to the health questions, weight, mood check-ins, your fasting activity, warning signs or health matters you write in a note, and prayer requests about health.

  • Health answers, weight and check-ins never leave your phone.
  • A note and your prayer topics are sent to the AI provider only for a personal prayer you asked for, after your consent.
  • Your group sees that you are fasting as a count, your name only if you choose. What you post to your group, your group sees.

We collect and share this data only to provide the features you use. We do not sell consumer health data. You can see, export and delete it in the app, withdraw consent in Profile, or write to us.

10. Children

Not By Bread is for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

11. Security

All connections are encrypted. Journal backups are stored encrypted. Access rules in our database let each person reach only their own data and the groups they belong to.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and tell you in the app when the change matters to you.
