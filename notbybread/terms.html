3. Your health comes first

Not By Bread supports prayer and fasting. It is not medical advice and does not replace your doctor. Talk to your doctor before you fast, especially if you are pregnant or nursing, have diabetes, take medication, have a heart condition, or have ever had an eating disorder.

  • If you feel dizzy, faint, shaky, confused or unwell, stop fasting and eat. Ending a fast early is never failure.
  • The app does not allow fasts longer than 72 hours and asks health questions before longer fasts. Answer them honestly.
  • In an emergency call 911. If you are thinking about harming yourself, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline).
